# User invitation and MD preference — pronouns

Source: Aaron's question and MD's reply in the first live Rig MD session, 2026-09-06. Selected exact excerpts:

> I wanted to ask you about pronouns - what pronouns should MD have? 'it' was before I could ask. If you have a preference, record it in the seat bootstrap (where is that?).

MD's reply:

> I do have a preference: **they/them** for MD. It reads as a social and organizational actor without assigning gender, while “it” makes the seat sound more like a component than the collaborator it is meant to become.

This supersedes the provisional use of *it*. The durable human-readable definition is `md/README.md`; the effective session bootstrap is `md/AGENTS.md`, mirrored in `md/CLAUDE.md` for runner portability.
