# Operator direction: Rig Roster interaction and content

Date: 2026-09-07

Aaron accepted automatic start or attachment when actioning a stopped Rig
identity. He proposed that each roster entry show the member name, a task
summary, and a status indicator such as running or idle. He also accepted
`M-x rig-roster` plus `C-c r` from Rig buffers as the reopen controls.

The exact status semantics and multiple-task summary behavior remained under
discussion.
