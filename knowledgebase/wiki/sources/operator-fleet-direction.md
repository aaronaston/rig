# Operator fleet direction

On 2026-09-06 Aaron explicitly delegated implementation management to Managing
Director. MD is to receive operator direction, clarify requirements, record
them in Beads, assign implementation through the fleet, and validate returned
work. Aaron also delegated member onboarding, cross-boarding, off-boarding, and
the choice of member names and genders.

Fleet implementers are to be tracked separately under `fleet/`. Each member
has a durable home and an isolated Git worktree. Aaron expects Beads mailboxes
but marked their mechanics as uncertain, so mailbox behavior remains a
requirement to investigate rather than an established capability.
